# Research

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science degree in Security and Resilience within the Department of Physics at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), thanks to the generous scholarship of the [Institute for Security Science and Technology](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/security-institute/). 

My current research is focused on exploring the security, safety, and resilience implications of diabetes technology. Specifically, I am examining the cybersecurity aspects of Continuous/Interstitial Glucose Monitoring Systems (CGMS), Blood Glucose Monitoring Systems (BGMS), Closed Loop Systems (APSs), Smart Insulin Pen (SIPs), and Smart Insulin Pump (SIPs) and other Point of Care (POC) IoT devices for diabetes treatment. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities to expand my knowledge and expertise in the cybersecurity aspects of diabetes devices. If you are interested in collaborating or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

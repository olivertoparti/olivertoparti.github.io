I am a security researcher and engineer working at the intersection of AI-enabled systems and cyber-physical infrastructure. My work spans applied research on connected medical device security, health technology policy, and the engineering of secure software in regulated environments.

I hold an MSc in Security and Resilience from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), awarded with Distinction and supported by a scholarship from the [Institute for Security Science and Technology](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/security-institute/), and a First Class BSc in Cybersecurity from Coventry University.

## Research

My research focuses on the cybersecurity of connected medical devices and the AI-enabled systems that increasingly surround them. I am the lead and corresponding author of [*Cybersecurity in Connected Medical Devices: A Policy Agenda for the NHS*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02534-4), published in *npj Digital Medicine* (Nature Portfolio) in March 2026 with Kunal Rajput, Ara Darzi and Saira Ghafur. The paper proposes a cross-layer risk framework spanning the physical, network and cloud layers of NHS device infrastructure, with recommendations to the MHRA on secure-by-design development, Software Bills of Materials and clear accountability.

I am currently co-leading a systematic review of cybersecurity interventions in diabetes technology, covering continuous glucose monitoring systems and insulin pumps, registered on PROSPERO as [CRD42025639477](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/display_record.php?RecordID=639477). The work brings together the Institute for Security Science and Technology and the [Institute of Global Health Innovation](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/global-health-innovation/) at Imperial College London.

I am seeking doctoral opportunities on the security of AI-enabled medical devices, and I welcome conversations with prospective supervisors, collaborators and clinical partners.

## Industry and public service

Alongside research, I am the founder of [CYBNODE Ltd](https://www.cybnode.com), a London-based AI product security consultancy delivering secure software and DevSecOps services, where I lead the development of Stravok, a platform that reduces audit effort for GDPR and ISO 27001. As Lead Engineer, E-commerce Platform at First Class Watches, I re-platformed a legacy e-commerce business onto a modern Next.js, Laravel and FastAPI stack, run its production infrastructure, and build LLM-based tooling for product search and internal analytics.

In 2025 and 2026 I co-organised programme development for the [Advanced Research + Invention Agency](https://www.aria.org.uk/)'s *Trust Everything, Everywhere* initiative, contributing expertise in cyber-physical systems and secure AI. I also serve on the Alumni Advisory Board of the College of Engineering, Environment and Science at Coventry University.

## Subheadings use two hashes

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. Links look like [this](https://toparti.com).
Images go in `/images/` and are embedded with `![Alt text](/images/filename.jpg)`.

To publish: copy this file to `_posts/` and name it `YYYY-MM-DD-your-post-slug.md`,
with the date matching the `date:` field. Files in `_drafts/` never appear on the site.

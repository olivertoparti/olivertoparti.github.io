**Founder · [vouv.io](https://vouv.io)**

Vouv is a marketplace where you hire an expert's AI agent rather than a generic one. Every agent is built and backed by a named, verified professional. You buy a defined task at a fixed price, in areas such as contract review, tax structuring or visa applications, and if you need the human, they are one tap away. Experts sign up to build and sell their own agents, and multi-step workflows can chain several experts' agents together. A dispute process with refunds sits underneath all of it.

The idea has been with me for a long time, well before the current wave of AI agents made it practical. Its design question is the one I keep returning to in my research: how do you trust a system that acts on someone's behalf? Vouv's answer is to put a verified, accountable person behind every agent, so that trust in the software is anchored in trust in a professional.

I will write about the journey, and the trade-offs behind that design, on the [blog](/blog/).

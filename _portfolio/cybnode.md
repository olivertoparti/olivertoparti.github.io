**Founder · July 2021 to present · Bishopsgate, London · [cybnode.com](https://www.cybnode.com)**

CYBNODE began as a web development business during the COVID-19 pandemic and has grown into a consultancy based in Bishopsgate, London, focused on the security of AI products. We review AI systems for security gaps before clients and regulators find them, and we build and secure AI products across every layer from day one. Between 2021 and 2023 it built websites and web applications for more than 25 clients.

The current focus is **Stravok**, CYBNODE's DevSecOps platform. Stravok is built for AI products and reduces the effort of staying audit-ready for GDPR and ISO 27001, so that small teams can meet the security reviews that enterprise and finance customers demand.

Running CYBNODE is where much of my thinking on secure-by-design development and accountability comes from. The same questions I ask in [my research](/) on connected medical devices apply directly to the AI products the consultancy works on.
